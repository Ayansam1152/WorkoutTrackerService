## Fitness & Workout Knowledge

### Workout Types
- **Cardio:** Running, cycling, swimming, jumping rope, etc.
- **Strength:** Bodyweight exercises (push-ups, squats, planks), weightlifting, resistance bands.
- **Flexibility:** Stretching, yoga, Pilates, mobility drills.
- **HIIT:** High-Intensity Interval Training alternates bursts of hard effort and recovery.

### Key Training Principles
- **Warm Up/Cool Down:** Always do a 5–10 minute warm-up and cool-down.
- **FITT Principle:** Frequency, Intensity, Time, Type—apply these to structure any routine.
- **Progressive Overload:** Gradually increase weight, reps, sets, or intensity as you improve.

### Tracking & Metrics
- Record your workouts: sets, reps, weights, duration.
- Track rest, sleep, and recovery for better results.

### Workout Routines
- **Full-body routines:** Target all muscle groups (3x per week for beginners).
- **Split routines:** Separate muscle groups (e.g., Push/Pull/Legs or Upper/Lower).

### Nutrition for Fitness
- **Macronutrients:** Balance protein, carbohydrates, and fats according to fitness goals.
- **Hydration:** Aim for 2–3 liters of water daily.
- **Pre/Post-Workout:** Eat complex carbs & protein before/after exercise.

### Safety & General Advice
- Maintain proper form to prevent injury.
- Rest between sets (typically 1–2 minutes).
- Allow muscles at least 48 hours to recover between sessions of the same group.
